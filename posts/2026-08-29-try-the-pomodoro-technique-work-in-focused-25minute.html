## The Core Strategy: Focus in 25-Minute Increments

The Pomodoro Technique involves working in focused 25-minute increments, followed by a 5-minute break. Here's why it works:

  • Boosts concentration: Regular breaks help you recharge and come back to your task with renewed energy and focus.
  • Increases sense of accomplishment: Completing a series of focused sessions gives you a sense of accomplishment and motivation to tackle the next task.
  • Reduces distractions: By dedicating a set time to a task, you're less likely to get sidetracked by non-essential activities.

Here's how to implement the Pomodoro Technique:

  • Set a timer for 25 minutes
  • Work on your task without any interruptions or distractions
  • Take a 5-minute break when the timer goes off
  • Repeat the cycle for a total of 4-6 rounds
  • Take a longer break of 15-30 minutes after completing the rounds
