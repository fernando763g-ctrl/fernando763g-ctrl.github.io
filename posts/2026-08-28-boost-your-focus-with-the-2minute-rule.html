## The 2-Minute Rule: A Simple yet Powerful Strategy

The 2-Minute Rule is straightforward:

  • If a task can be done in less than 2 minutes, do it immediately
  • This means tackling small, insignificant tasks that often get pushed aside due to their perceived insignificance
  • By doing these tasks right away, you'll eliminate the mental clutter, free up space in your schedule, and build momentum for more significant tasks

Examples of tasks that fit the 2-Minute Rule include:

  • Responding to a simple email
  • Updating a to-do list
  • Saving a document to the cloud
  • Taking a quick note

By implementing the 2-Minute Rule, you'll develop a habit of tackling small tasks immediately, which will:

  • Save time in the long run by avoiding procrastination
  • Build your mental energy and focus
  • Create a sense of accomplishment and motivation
