**Focus is not about doing more things**

2026-08-12

**The Focus Myth: Doing Less to Achieve More**

We're often told that focus is about doing more things more efficiently. But the truth is, **focus is not about quantity**, but about quality. It's about eliminating the non-essential and concentrating on what truly matters.

### Prioritize with Purpose

Before you start tackling your tasks, ask yourself: "Is this essential to my goals?" Be ruthless in your assessment. If a task doesn't align with your objectives, **don't waste time on it**.

* Use tools like Notion or Todoist to categorize tasks by priority and goal alignment. * Set clear deadlines for non-essential tasks to avoid procrastination.

### Eliminate Distractions

Identify common distractions that derail your focus:

* Social media notifications * Email checks throughout the day * Meetings without a clear agenda

* Implement website blockers like Freedom or SelfControl to limit access during focused work sessions. * Schedule email checks to specific times of the day (e.g., morning and afternoon). * Establish a "no meeting" day each week.

### Focus on the Essentials

Once you've eliminated non-essential tasks and distractions, focus on what truly matters:

* Break down large goals into smaller, manageable tasks. * Use tools like ClickUp to create a project schedule and track progress. * Set aside dedicated time for focused work (e.g., 90 minutes + 10-minute break).

### Review and Adjust

Regularly review your priorities and task list:

* Reflect on what's working and what's not. * Adjust your schedule as needed.

By following these steps, you'll be able to cut out distractions, eliminate non-essential tasks, and focus on what truly matters. Remember, **focus is a skill that takes practice**. Start by doing less, but focusing on what counts.
