Before diving headfirst into the world of productivity apps, take a step back and ask yourself: what do I want to achieve? What are my goals?

  • Be specific: Instead of 'be more productive', try 'finish my project within the next 6 weeks'.
  • Make it measurable: Quantify your goals, so you can track your progress.
  • Keep it realistic: Don't set yourself up for failure by setting unattainable goals.

Once you have a clear direction, you can start selecting tools that support your goals, rather than the other way around.

Create a Framework for Success

A solid framework is essential for maintaining focus and making progress towards your goals. Here's how to create one:

  • Prioritize: Identify the most critical tasks that need to be completed, and tackle them first.
  • Schedule: Allocate specific time slots for each task, and stick to them.
  • Review: Regularly review your progress, and adjust your schedule as needed.
