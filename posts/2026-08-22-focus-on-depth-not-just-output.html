**Focus on Depth, Not Just Output** 📊

We often measure productivity by how much work gets done in a day. But is that really the best metric? If you're constantly churning out low-quality work, are you truly being productive? 🤔

**Prioritize Depth over Output** 📊

When you focus solely on output, you may sacrifice quality and impact. Instead, prioritize tasks that have a significant impact on your goals. Ask yourself:

"What one task can I do today that will..." * Move a key project forward by 10%? * Solve a critical problem? * Create something of lasting value?

**Use a "Done" List to Track Depth** 📝

A "done" list can be a powerful tool for tracking the depth of your work. Unlike a to-do list, which can be overwhelming and feel like a never-ending task list, a "done" list shows what you've accomplished. Write down each task you complete, and highlight or label the ones that had significant impact.

For example, in Todoist, you can create a "Done" tag to track your completed tasks. Then, regularly review your "done" list to identify patterns and areas for improvement.

**Schedule Time for Depth Work** ⏰

Make time for tasks that require focus and concentration. Set aside blocks of uninterrupted time, free from meetings and distractions. Use tools like Notion to schedule these blocks and protect them from overcommitting.

**Measure Progress, Not Just Output** 📈

Finally, redefine what success looks like. Instead of measuring productivity by the number of tasks completed, focus on the progress you make towards your goals. Use metrics like:

* The number of "done" tasks that move a project forward * The quality of your output (e.g., fewer errors, more engagement) * The impact of your work on others (e.g., positive feedback, increased collaboration)

By focusing on depth, not just output, you'll produce higher-quality work, make a greater impact, and ultimately become more productive.
