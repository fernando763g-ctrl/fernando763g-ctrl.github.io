### Are You a "Morning Person" by Design or Default?

We've all been there - scrolling through social media, seeing posts about "becoming a morning person" and feeling like we're failing at life because we're not naturally inclined to wake up at 5 am. **Your Schedule is a Reflection of Your Habits**

The way you approach your day is a result of your habits, not the other way around. If you're always rushing to get work done and feel like you're constantly playing catch-up, it's likely because you're not prioritizing effectively. Take a step back and examine your schedule. Are you leaving enough time for focused work, or are you constantly switching between tasks? 💡
