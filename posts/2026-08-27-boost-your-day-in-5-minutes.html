Clear Your Inbox: Delete or Schedule Non-Essential Emails

Inbox clutter can be a major productivity killer. To clear your inbox in 5 minutes, follow these steps:

  • Delete any emails that are no longer relevant or can be dealt with later.
  • Use the "2-minute rule": if an email can be dealt with in less than 2 minutes, do it immediately.
  • Schedule non-essential emails to be dealt with at a later time, such as during your designated "email management" slot.

Prioritize Your Top 3 Tasks for the Day

To focus on what truly matters, prioritize your top 3 tasks for the day. Follow these steps:

  • Write down your top 3 tasks for the day in a dedicated notebook or task management app.
  • Set clear goals and deadlines for each task.
  • Focus on completing these high-priority tasks first, and then move on to less important tasks.
