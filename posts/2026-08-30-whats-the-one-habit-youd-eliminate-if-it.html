The One Habit to Cut for an Extra Hour of Productivity Each Day 🚀

Are you tired of feeling like you're drowning in a sea of tasks, meetings, and distractions? Do you struggle to focus on what really matters, and find yourself losing precious time to procrastination, social media, or email? You're not alone. We all face the same challenges, but it's time to take control.

Identify the Time Suckers

Before we dive into what habit to eliminate, let's identify the common culprits that drain our productivity:

  • Social media and email notifications
  • Multitasking and switching between tasks
  • Meetings and unnecessary communication
  • Procrastination and perfectionism

To gain an extra hour of productivity each day, we need to eliminate one of these habits. But which one? Consider the following:

## Eliminate Multitasking

Multitasking may seem like a productivity superpower, but it's actually a major time thief. When you switch between tasks, you lose:

  • Focus and momentum
  • Time due to context switching
  • Quality of work due to divided attention

Try this: Focus on a single task for 90 minutes, then take a 10-minute break. This technique is called the Pomodoro Technique, and it can help you stay focused and avoid multitasking.

## Prioritize with Intent

Prioritization is key to productivity, but it's easy to get caught up in urgent tasks at the expense of important ones. To prioritize with intent:

  • Use the Eisenhower Matrix: categorize tasks into urgent vs. important, and focus on the most critical ones first
  • Set clear goals and objectives: ensure each task aligns with your goals and priorities
  • Use a task management system: like Todoist Pro, which we'll discuss next
