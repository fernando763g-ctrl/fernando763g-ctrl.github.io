The #1 Habit that Boosted My Productivity

As a productivity coach, I've worked with numerous clients who struggle to form lasting habits. But one question always gets them thinking: "What's the #1 habit I've formed in the past year that's had the most significant impact on my productivity?"

Creating a Daily Schedule

For me, it was establishing a consistent daily schedule. I used to be a night owl, but I realized that this was affecting my energy levels and ability to focus. So, I started waking up at 6:00 AM every day. I allocate the first two hours for deep work, followed by a 30-minute break for exercise and meditation.

Implementing the 2-Minute Rule

Another game-changer was implementing the 2-minute rule. If a task can be done in less than 2 minutes, I do it immediately. This has saved me so much time in the long run, as I'm not constantly switching between tasks and losing momentum.

Using a Habit Tracker

To stay on track, I use a habit tracker in Notion. I log my daily habits, including my daily schedule, exercise, and meditation practice. Seeing my progress has been a huge motivator, and it's helped me identify areas where I need to improve.

Eliminating Multitasking

Lastly, I've eliminated multitasking from my daily routine. I used to think I could get more done by doing multiple things at once, but research has shown that this is actually a productivity killer. Now, I focus on one task at a time, and I'm amazed at how much more efficient I've become.

Conclusion

Forming lasting habits takes time and effort, but the payoff is worth it. By creating a daily schedule, implementing the 2-minute rule, using a habit tracker, and eliminating multitasking, I've been able to boost my productivity and achieve my goals.
